Scientific Pillars & Groundwork

1. Quantum Unitarity & Landauer's Principle

In modern physics, quantum unitarity guarantees that total probability and quantum information are strictly conserved. Rolf Landauer proved that information is inherently physical. When biological decay occurs, environmental decoherence scatters particle configurations across the environment, but the underlying data cannot be deleted from the universe.

Pillar Science: Landauer’s Principle (1961), Quantum No-Hiding Theorem & Black Hole Information Paradox Resolution.

2. Quantum Biology & Photosynthesis

Groundbreaking experiments on the Fenna-Matthews-Olson (FMO) complex demonstrated that living organic matter utilizes quantum coherence in warm, wet environments to transfer energy at near 100% efficiency. Organic systems naturally maintain quantum states across biological interfaces.

Pillar Science: Quantum Coherence in Biological Systems (Nature, FMO Complex Studies).

3. Bio-Electric Fungal Communication

Research led by Dr. Suzanne Simard proved that trees exchange carbon, nitrogen, and signals through mycorrhizal fungal networks. Furthermore, studies on fungal electrical activity demonstrate action-potential spikes similar to animal neural impulses, turning soil ecosystems into planetary-scale information processors.

Pillar Science: Isotopic Tracing ($^{14}\text{C}$ / $^{15}\text{N}$) & Andrew Adamatzky’s Fungal Bio-Electricity Studies.
